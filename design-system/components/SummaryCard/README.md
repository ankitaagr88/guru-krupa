A short standing brief on a returning patient, at the top of the drawer.

**When to use.** Only when there is history worth stating — a previous visit, ongoing drops, a chronic condition. A first-time patient gets no summary card; an empty one is worse than none.

**What the consumer provides.** The title and two to four `summary-line`s, each a fact with its key term in `<b>`. Keep them to one line each.

**Anatomy.** `sapphire-wash` ground, `radius-lg`, `sapphire-line` border, an uppercase `eyebrow` title in `sapphire-deep` with 0.6px tracking — the only uppercase run in the product, which is what makes it read as a standing note rather than live data. Bold terms inside lines take `sapphire-deep`.

**Content, not chrome.** Write the lines a nurse would say handing the file over: *Last visit 42 days ago*, *On Timolol and Latanoprost*, *Diabetes, Hypertension*. Not counts, not percentages, not anything the drawer already shows in full below.

**Border and ground agree.** `sapphire-line` on `sapphire-wash` are cut from the same ramp. The prototype's version of this card had a green-grey border on a blue-grey ground — a leftover from a palette migration it never finished, and one of the things the refresh cleaned up.
